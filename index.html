Personal agent harness

Personal agent harness is a single-user, self-hosted tool. It lets its owner's own personal AI assistant read the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar, and prepare drafts or events that the owner approves one by one.

It runs only on the owner's computer. It is not a public service, has no other users, and is not offered to anyone else.

Every capability is off until the owner turns it on, and every action that changes something needs the owner's explicit approval.
