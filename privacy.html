Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Personal agent harness is a single-user, self-hosted tool used only by its owner, who connects their own Google account to their own computer. This page explains what happens to Google user data in that setup.

What the app accesses

Only the Google data the owner chooses to allow, limited to the permissions (scopes) the owner approves on Google's consent screen, for example read access to Gmail or Google Calendar. Each capability must also be granted separately inside the app.

How it is used

Google data is used only to provide the features the owner asked for: searching mail, listing calendar events, and preparing drafts or events that the owner approves. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone else.

Where it is stored

The app runs on the owner's computer. Sign-in credentials are kept in the operating system's secure keychain. Content that is read is not uploaded to any server operated by the app's author. A redacted activity log (what was requested and when, not message contents) is kept on the owner's computer.

Sharing with an AI model provider

The owner's assistant may send specific Google data to an AI model provider chosen by the owner, to answer the owner's request. This happens only after the owner has given consent for that provider and that category of data, and the owner can withdraw consent at any time. Such data is handled under that provider's own terms.

Human access

No person other than the owner reads the owner's Google data.

Control and deletion

The owner can disconnect Google at any time in the app, which deletes the stored credentials, and can revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local data folder removes everything it stored.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
